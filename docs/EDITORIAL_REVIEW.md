# Editorial review register

All 12 initial stories are complete **unpublished drafts**. The creation timestamp is 8 November 2026. Publication timestamps are deliberately null until actual approval; an invented publication date would misrepresent their status. Reading times are derived from body word counts.

The byline is the Spot Magazine editorial desk, an organisational attribution. No named journalist, interview, credential, visit or first-hand experience is claimed. Drafts offer practical editorial suggestions, not medical/financial/legal advice or current celebrity claims.

## Reference checks performed during preparation

- GOV.UK local library finder: retrieved; supports its England/Wales scope and links to local services. Individual branch services still need checking.
- VisitBritain things-to-do directory: retrieved; used as a discovery reference, with venue-specific conditions left for readers/reviewers to confirm.
- National Trust visit directory: retrieved; no individual property's prices, opening hours or access facilities are claimed.
- V&A South Kensington visitor page: official search result retrieved; a detailed page retrieval was not available. The reviewer must reopen and verify current admission/exhibition arrangements before publishing.
- RHS herbs-in-containers guide: official search result retrieved; detailed retrieval was unavailable. Draft deliberately defers species-specific watering and growing details to the source and plant label. Reviewer confirmation remains required.
- Official London Theatre access directory: official search result retrieved; services and actual performance dates must be checked with each venue. No performance date or price is invented.
- BFI Player: retrieved; subscription, rental and free collections observed. No specific title's ongoing availability or price is promised.
- NCSC online-security collection: retrieved; the draft refers only to basic software-update and account-protection guidance and links the original.
- Ofcom media-literacy resources: retrieved; cited as an educational starting point, not validation of a specific social post.

## Reviewer checklist for every article

1. Read the entire draft for usefulness, specificity, clarity and British English.
2. Verify each externally checkable claim against its linked original source. Check current dates/availability where relevant. Identify advice as an editorial suggestion.
3. Confirm every internal link resolves to a reviewed published article at release time. Related drafts should be published together or links removed until their targets are live.
4. Confirm original artwork is approved or obtain documented image rights. Check alt text and avoid implying illustrations depict actual photographed events.
5. Review title, description, headings and author attribution. Add correction/disclosure notes where applicable.
6. Record reviewer identity, review date, source/image checks and corrections in the CMS review note. Tick factual review and separately approve publication/scheduling.
7. Review the resulting branch diff and production deployment independently. Source checking during drafting does not replace a human editorial sign-off.
